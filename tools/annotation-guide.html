annotating a score

Caplin sets out how an analysis is written onto a score: what goes above the staff, what goes below, and which marks carry which meaning. The eight bars here are annotated by those rules. Turn a layer off to see what the score looks like without it, and press a rule to find its mark.

Press any rule on the left to light the marks it governs. The four switches turn the annotation layers on and off. The numeral switch moves the harmonic labels between Caplin's system and the one this course uses.
