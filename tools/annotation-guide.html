annotating a score

Caplin sets out how an analysis is written onto a score: what goes above the staff, what goes below, and what each mark means. Caplin's own Example 2.1 is annotated here by those rules. Turn a layer off to see what the score looks like without it, and press a rule to find its mark.

Press any rule on the left to light the marks it governs. The switches turn the annotation layers on and off and move the harmonic labels between Caplin's system and the one this course uses. In a minor key the two part company on almost every chord.
