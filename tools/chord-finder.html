chord finder

Enter the notes of a chord from the bottom up and read what they make. The chord builder goes from a root and a recipe to a chord. This goes the other way: from notes on a page to a quality, a root, an inversion, and, in a key, a roman numeral and a function.

Choose an accidental, then a letter, to add a note. Notes go in from the bass up, to a maximum of four. Backspace removes the last one. Set a tonic and a mode to read the chord in a key.
