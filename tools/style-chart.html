style chart

Western art music from the Carolingian chant reform to 1945, in three levels: era, period, and style or school. Each style has its dates, up to four of its composers, and the vocal and instrumental genres and forms it cultivated. Neighboring styles overlap in time, and their dates show where. Eras, dates, and terms follow Paul Griffiths, A Concise History of Western Music (Cambridge University Press, 2006), and the last column gives his pages.

Press an era to light its rows; press it again to clear. The column buttons show and hide columns. The chart prints on landscape pages.
