5, diatonic sequences

diatonic sequences

Realize each sequence in four voices, in keyboard style or in SATB, whichever you prefer, and keep to one of them across all three. The pattern that fits the first link fits every link after it: work out the voice leading once and carry it through. The three are the descending fifths, the ascending 5–6, and the descending seconds in first inversion.

A sequence suspends the syntax. Name what the vii° in the descending fifths is doing there, and which of its tones is doubled.
