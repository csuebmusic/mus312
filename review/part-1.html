7, nonchord tones

nonchord tones

Name the type in each situation.

  1. Over a tonic held through the bar in C major, the soprano moves E, F, G, with the F on a weak beat.
  2. Over V in C major, the soprano moves B, C, B, with the C on a weak beat.
  3. IV moves to V in C major. The soprano holds C across the change and falls to B on the second beat.
  4. Over a tonic in C major, the soprano moves G, F, E, with the F on the downbeat.
  5. At a cadence in C major, the soprano leaves D over the V and reaches C before the tonic arrives.
  6. The bass holds G while the upper voices move through I, ii6, and V.
