8, six realizations

figured bass · basic voice leading rules · nonchord tones

Everything here is diatonic. Keep the sensitive tones resolving, avoid parallel fifths and octaves, and keep the upper voices within an octave of each other. Where a line calls for one, write in a nonchord tone: a passing tone between chord tones a third apart, a neighbor over a repeated one, a suspension across a change of harmony.
